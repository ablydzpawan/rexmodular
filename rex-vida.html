Smart Access. Connected Living. Future Ready.

Rex Vida is Rex Modular's intelligent building experience platform, designed to connect residents, property managers, and buildings through a seamless digital ecosystem. As modular construction continues to redefine how buildings are delivered, Rex Vida redefines how people interact with those buildings. From secure access and visitor management to resident communication and property operations, Rex Vida brings modern living into a single connected platform.

Built for
Future Living

Modern residents expect more than just a place to live—they expect convenience, connectivity, and control. Rex Vida transforms residential communities into smart living environments by integrating building access, resident services, and property management tools into one streamlined experience.

For Residents

  • Mobile-first building access
  • Digital guest passes and visitor management
  • Community announcements and notifications
  • Amenity reservations
  • Secure and convenient everyday living

For Property Managers

  • Centralized access management
  • Resident communication tools
  • Building activity monitoring
  • Streamlined operational workflows
  • Reduced administrative burden

For Owners & Developers

  • Enhanced resident experience
  • Increased operational efficiency
  • Scalable across portfolios
  • Future-ready technology infrastructure
  • Designed to integrate with leading property management platforms
