Rex Modular was founded by a team with extensive experience delivering complex high-rise, multifamily, and large-scale residential developments.

Today, we are applying that experience to a new generation of modular construction—designed specifically for the realities of dense urban development.

Our projects reflect both the experience behind Rex Modular and our vision for what high-rise modular construction can become.

3rd & Battery

Seattle, Washington

3rd & Battery is a planned 17-story, 165-home residential high-rise in downtown Seattle and is being developed as a flagship application of the Rex Tower System.

The project is designed to combine a five-story concrete podium with 12 stories of volumetric modular construction above, creating a hybrid building strategy tailored to the demands of high-density urban development.

The concrete podium is planned to accommodate the building’s urban interface, parking, circulation, and core infrastructure, while the residential tower above will utilize a highly repeatable modular system. This approach is intended to capture the efficiencies of off-site manufacturing while maintaining the flexibility required for a complex downtown site.

Architecture, structural engineering, MEP systems, manufacturing, logistics, and installation are being coordinated early in the design process to create an integrated path from design and factory production through delivery and on-site assembly.

As the project advances, 3rd & Battery is intended to demonstrate how the Rex Tower System can extend volumetric modular construction beyond conventional low- and mid-rise applications and into the high-rise environment.

The project represents what Rex Modular is working to make possible: urban, high-density development delivered through a more integrated, predictable, and scalable approach to building.

Belmare Tower

Seattle, Washington

Belmare Tower represents the high-rise development experience behind Rex Modular.

Located in Seattle’s Belltown neighborhood, the 24-story residential tower is being developed in one of the city’s most prominent urban settings, with views toward Elliott Bay, the Olympic Mountains, and Olympic Sculpture Park.

The project reflects the team’s experience navigating the full lifecycle of high-rise development—from site planning, design, entitlement, and consultant coordination to construction strategy, financing, and long term operations.

While Belmare Tower is a conventionally constructed development rather than a modular project, experience delivering projects of this scale directly informs the thinking behind Rex Modular: understanding what developers, architects, engineers, contractors, lenders, and operators need to successfully execute complex urban buildings.

That developer-first perspective is fundamental to how Rex Modular approaches the next generation of high-rise construction.

Rainier Landing

SeaTac, Washington

Rainier Landing demonstrates the large-scale multifamily development experience behind Rex Modular.

Located in SeaTac’s Angle Lake District, this transit-oriented, master-planned community is transforming approximately six acres of former light-industrial property into a high-density residential destination near the Angle Lake Light Rail Station.

The two-phase development is planned to deliver 726 residences, with 355 residences in Phase I, supported by landscaped outdoor spaces, resident amenities, convenient transit access, and views of Puget Sound and the surrounding mountains.

Members of the team behind Rex Modular bring direct experience from Synergy Construction & Development, which is involved in the development and delivery of Rainier Landing.

That experience—from design coordination and entitlement through construction, operations, and long term ownership considerations—helps shape Rex Modular around the practical needs of developers delivering complex projects at scale.
