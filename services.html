Every Stage of Modular
Under One Roof

One team coordinates every stage—from early feasibility and design to manufacturing, logistics, and final installation.

01

Feasibility Study

Can modular work for your project?

Our feasibility study answers that question before you make major commitments.

We evaluate the site, development program, design requirements, logistics, cost considerations, and schedule to determine whether high-rise modular construction is suitable for the opportunity.

The Feasibility Process

  • Zoning analysis
  • Site and development program review
  • Preliminary floor plans
  • Parking strategy
  • Unit mix and yield study
  • 3D massing
  • Preliminary cost and schedule analysis
  • Modular design and logistics considerations
  • Key project risks
  • Recommended timeline
  • Consultant team requirements and qualifications
02

Design & Development

Turning a feasible opportunity into a buildable project

Once feasibility has been established, Rex Modular helps turn the opportunity into a coordinated, buildable project.

We provide project integration and oversight throughout design and development, keeping the owner, consultant team, manufacturer, and general contractor aligned.

Design & Development Services

  • Entitlement process oversight
  • Architectural, structural, and MEP design coordination
  • Consultant team oversight
  • Modular planning and design integration
  • Cost and budget development with the general contractor
  • Project scheduling and milestone management
  • Manufacturer integration
  • General contractor and subcontractor coordination
  • Construction lender coordination
03

Pre-Construction & Installation

Preparing the building, site, and team for delivery

A successful installation depends on the readiness of the building, the site, the modules, and every team involved.

Rex Modular works with the owner, general contractor, manufacturer, and subcontractors to prepare for a coordinated factory-to-site delivery.

Pre-construction & Installation Services

  • Owner and general contractor coordination
  • Site-readiness planning
  • Quality assurance and quality control
  • Foundation, podium, and building-core coordination
  • Utility and connection coordination
  • Manufacturing interface and progress oversight
  • Transportation and logistics planning
  • Module delivery sequencing
  • Installation and subcontractor coordination

Integration From the Outset

Rex Modular coordinates every phase of modular construction, from the initial site study through final module installation.
